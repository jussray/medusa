THE STONE TEST

Challenge the assumptions.

The Medusa method tests consequential decisions. This preview describes the method; it does not monitor networks or run threat scans.

01 · Attack

Examine premises, assumptions, evidence, authority and reversibility.

02 · Adversary

Consider outsiders, insiders and untrusted input as sources of failure.

03 · Stone test

Identify the assumption that could invalidate the decision.

04–05 · Rebuttal and final cut

Challenge findings, rank surviving risks and record a verdict.
